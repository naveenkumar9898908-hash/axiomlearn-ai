🚀 Simple 4-Step Architecture

How AxiomLearn AI Works (Step-by-Step)

🟦 STEP 01

Step 1: Practice

The student first solves interactive questions.

The AI automatically changes the difficulty of the questions.

  • • If questions are too easy, AI makes them harder.
  • • If too difficult, AI makes them easier.
  • • Goal: Keep student in the “flow zone” — challenging but not frustrating.
💡 Example: If learning Java and you easily answer basic questions, AI gives slightly harder problems.
👉 Purpose: Give the student the right level of practice.
🟪 STEP 02

Step 2: AI Listens

While answering, AI observes student performance:

  • • Number of correct answers
  • • Time taken per question
  • • Quick mistakes vs. long conceptual struggles
< 3s: Quick / careless mistake.
> 30s: Deeper difficulty with concept.
💡 Example: Accidentally misclicking vs spending 40s confused are treated differently.
👉 Purpose: Understand why the student is struggling.
🟨 STEP 03

Step 3: Detective

If stuck, AI acts like a detective, scanning the family tree of concepts:

Advanced ➔ Basic ➔ Prerequisite
Example: Arrays ➔ Loops ➔ Variables.
Struggling with arrays? The real issue is often loops.

Instead of repeating array questions, AI identifies the foundational topic you need first.

👉 Purpose: Find the root cause of the confusion.
🟩 STEP 04

Step 4: 60s Fix

AI provides 4 quick learning activities to heal the gap:

  • 1. Refresher Card: Short, friendly concept recap.
  • 2. Root Checkup: Small probe on basic rule.
  • 3. Bridge Problem: Gradually connects basic to advanced.
  • 4. Victory Test: Confetti celebration with XP rewards! 🎉
👉 Purpose: Quickly repair gaps & get back on track.
