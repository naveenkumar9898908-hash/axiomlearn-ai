AxiomLearn AI Adaptive Knowledge Tracing & Prerequisite Graph Diagnostic Engine

Eliminating the uniform pacing crisis in classrooms by pinpointing foundational prerequisite misconceptions through real-time dual-engine AI (BKT + DKT), Zone of Proximal Development (2PL IRT) dynamic flow state matching, and automated 4-step targeted micro-practice remediation.

Pillar 01

Precision Diagnostic

Traverses Directed Acyclic Graph (DAG) syllabus prerequisites backwards to isolate foundational blockers from superficial student slip errors.

Pillar 02

Dual AI Tracing

Combines Bayesian Knowledge Tracing with latency-aware slip modulation & a 32-neuron Recurrent GRU (DKT) predicting multi-concept mastery transfers.

Pillar 03

Closed-Loop Mastery

Automates personalized 4-step micro-practice paths with gamification (XP, streaks, badges) and teacher cohort CRUD intelligence.

Problem Statement & Educational Scope

The Educational Crisis & Target Scope

⚠️ The Flaws of Traditional Instruction

  • •
    The Uniform Pacing Trap: Standard instruction moves at a fixed class tempo. Struggling students fall further behind, while fast learners get disengaged.
  • •
    Hidden Foundational Gaps: A student failing Calculus or Recursion is rarely blocked by the theorem itself—their roadblock is forgotten prerequisite Algebra or base cases.
  • •
    Lagging Diagnostic Feedback: Periodic exams provide post-mortem scores weeks late, with zero actionable path to heal the specific misconception.

🎯 Scope & Operational Boundaries

  • •
    Curricular Coverage: Comprehensive multi-tiered STEM graphs in Higher Mathematics (Calculus, Trigonometry) and Computer Science (Algorithms, Trees).
  • •
    Full-Stack Solution: Dynamic Cyber-HUD Practice Arena, Prerequisite DAG Explorer, BKT/DKT Sandbox, and Teacher Cohort CRUD Command Center.
  • •
    Zero Server Latency: In-browser edge computation executing IRT difficulty adaptation and Bayesian updates in <5 milliseconds.
System Workflow & Architecture

The 5-Stage Adaptive Cognitive Loop

STAGE 01

Telemetry Ingestion

Tracks response correctness, latency, and distractor signatures. Classifies fast (<3s) careless slips vs slow (>30s) conceptual struggles.

STAGE 02

Dual Knowledge Tracing

BKT evaluates Corbett-Anderson posterior P(L_t). Recurrent GRU (DKT) propagates multi-concept cognitive transfers across syllabus nodes.

STAGE 03

2PL IRT Flow Selection

Item Response Theory maps student ability θ against question difficulty b to lock learner in the Zone of Proximal Development (~70-75% success).

STAGE 04

Root-Cause Traversal

Upon persistent failure, backward DAG traversal investigates parent prerequisites to isolate the true foundational misconception.

STAGE 05

4-Step Micro-Remediation

Step 1: Refresher • Step 2: Foundational Check • Step 3: Scaffolded Bridge • Step 4: Mastery Verification with XP celebration.
